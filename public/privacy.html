Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Dayboard is a client-side dashboard application. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data we do not collect

Dayboard has no backend server and does not transmit any personal data to its developers. There are no analytics, crash-reporting trackers, or advertising networks embedded in the application.

2. Data stored locally

All your preferences, widget settings, and API tokens are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

3. Third-party services

When you connect an optional integration, your browser communicates directly with that service's API. Dayboard itself never sees or stores your credentials:

  • Google Calendar – OAuth tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage. Dayboard requests read-only access (calendar.readonly) only. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Spotify – OAuth tokens are stored locally. Spotify's privacy policy applies: spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • OpenWeatherMap – Your API key is stored locally. Weather requests include your configured city name or coordinates.
  • Calendar feeds (ICS/webcal) – Feed URLs you add are fetched via a CORS proxy bundled with the app. The proxy does not log requests.

4. Cookies

Dayboard does not use cookies.

5. Children's privacy

Dayboard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/vargamateistvan/dayboard/issues.
